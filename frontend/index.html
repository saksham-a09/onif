Everything you need to know about how Finovo manages pooled funds, generates trading returns, and distributes weekly profits to your wallet.

Finovo is an institutional crypto investment and profit-sharing platform. We pool capital from investors and deploy it across high-performing quantitative and algorithmic trading strategies. Investors earn structured weekly returns (ROI) based on their chosen plan up to a 300.00% maximum total return cap.
Our trading desk employs institutional-grade strategies including cross-exchange statistical arbitrage, high-frequency market making, momentum execution, and delta-neutral hedging to capture profits across major cryptocurrency markets regardless of broader market direction.
After completing standard KYC verification, select your desired plan (Starter, Pro, or Elite), transfer the investment amount in USDT via BEP20 (Binance Smart Chain) or TRC20 (TRON) to our official deposit address, and provide your transaction hash (TxID). Once verified by admin, your plan is immediately active.
Weekly ROI earnings are calculated and credited directly to your account balance every week. You can withdraw your spendable balance anytime in USDT with rapid turnaround and a flat $1 transaction fee.
Each investment package has a total earning limit of 300.00% of your initial deposited principal. Both weekly trading ROI and direct referral commissions fill your active plan toward this 300% ceiling. Once a plan completes, you may deposit into a new plan to continue earning.
Share your unique sponsor link to earn a 2.0% Direct Referral Commission on team deposits, plus 1.5% Weekly ROI Level Income up to 5 tiers deep based on your active direct members. Commissions credit automatically to accelerate your active investment plans.
All client funds are safeguarded in segregated institutional multi-signature cold wallets with strict internal dual-authorization controls. We mandate KYC verification for all investors to comply with global AML/CFT standards.
Support is available 24/7 directly through your member portal via the "Support Tickets" module. Submit a ticket regarding deposits, withdrawals, or general inquiries and our operations desk will resolve your request promptly.
