Our Commitments

Built on a Foundation
of Trust & Transparency

Every feature in Finovo is designed to protect your capital and keep you in control. We hold ourselves to institutional-grade standards — not retail minimums.

Direct API Execution

Your trades are routed directly to the exchange API without intermediary handling. Achieve complete control over your orders with zero platform-induced latency.

Non-Custodial Design

We never hold your funds. Finovo connects to your existing exchange wallets via API keys, keeping you in complete custody of your assets at all times.

Encrypted WebSockets

All data streams are secured using AES-256 encryption. Your API keys and trading strategies are securely locked in our institutional-grade vault infrastructure.

Latency Optimization

Our servers are colocated near major crypto exchanges to ensure sub-millisecond data delivery and trade execution. Never miss a market movement again.
